Acknowledgments in the episodes

At the end of episodes, we may thank the project’s supporters by showing their names. Your name will appear only if you give us your express permission (for example, by checking the corresponding box when you support the project, or by replying to our message). Agreeing to these terms does not count as giving that permission.

You can choose how you’d like to appear: with your full name, with your first name only, or with a pseudonym. If you don’t give your permission, your support is just as valuable, and your name will not be published.

You can withdraw your permission at any time by writing to info@bibliafilm.com. Your name will no longer appear in subsequent episodes, and we will remove it from episodes already published to the extent each platform allows.
