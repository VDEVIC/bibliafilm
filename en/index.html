THE PROJECT

A timeless story.
A new way
to experience it.

The idea is to bring the whole Bible to the screen, word for word, and share the film while it’s being made.

Bible Film is an independent project. It is based on the King James Version (1769) and uses artificial intelligence to create images, voice, and sound: an artistic interpretation of the text.
